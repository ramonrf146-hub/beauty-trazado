Unlike a skincare routine (where order gets debated a lot), the most common mistake in hair care is simpler: using everything together, every day, without understanding what each step actually does. This guide separates the functions and tells you when to use each product.

## Step 1: Cleansing (every wash)

If you have dandruff or an itchy scalp, don't use a generic shampoo — you need a real active ingredient. The [CeraVe 2-in-1 Dandruff Shampoo & Conditioner](/en/productos/B0DV44KXW4) in this ranking has 1% pyrithione zinc, and already comes with conditioner included.

If your scalp is fine and you want a complete routine from one brand, the shampoo from the [Bare Anatomy kit](/en/productos/B0DCZSSG8B) is the "all in one" option.

## Step 2: Conditioning (every wash, if you didn't use a 2-in-1)

If you washed with a shampoo that doesn't include conditioner, add one for general hydration — the [CeraVe Gentle Hydrating Conditioner](/en/productos/B0FWVVDNML) in this ranking works for any hair type, it's not specific to one problem.

## Step 3: Weekly treatment (1-2 times a week, not daily)

This is where accumulated damage gets repaired, not where day-to-day maintenance happens. The mask from the [Bare Anatomy kit](/en/productos/B0DCZSSG8B) serves this function — it's left on for a few minutes before rinsing, unlike a conditioner that rinses out quickly.

## Step 4: Heat protection (every time you use a flat iron or blow dryer)

This step is applied to **wet** hair, **before** any heat tool — using it afterward protects nothing. The [Olaplex N°.7 Bonding Hair Oil](/en/productos/B07VR1NDSQ) in this ranking protects up to 450°F and adds shine too.

## Step 5: Frizz and shine (on wet or dry hair, as a finishing touch)

The [Garnier Fructis Anti-Frizz Serum](/en/productos/B006J44BSI) in this ranking seals the cuticle to control frizz in humid climates — used in minimal drops, too much weighs hair down.

## Step 6: Between washes (a quick fix, not a substitute for an actual wash)

When there's no time to wash but hair looks oily, the [Batiste Dry Shampoo](/en/productos/B07MGZ6X2Q) in this ranking absorbs scalp oil without water — sprayed away from the roots, not directly onto them.

## The most common mistake: using everything, every day

None of these steps replaces another, but you also don't need to use all of them at every wash. A realistic routine looks like this:

- **Every day:** cleansing + conditioning (steps 1-2)
- **Before heat:** heat protection (step 4)
- **1-2 times a week:** deep treatment (step 3)
- **When needed:** frizz control (step 5) or dry shampoo between washes (step 6)

## Frequently asked questions

**Can I use the anti-dandruff shampoo and the hydrating conditioner together?**
Yes — the 2-in-1 shampoo already includes conditioner, but if your scalp ends up dry you can add the moisturizing conditioner as an extra step without issue.

**Does the heat-protecting oil replace the anti-frizz serum?**
Not entirely — the oil prioritizes heat protection and shine, the serum prioritizes frizz control in humid climates. You can use both: the oil before heat, the serum as a finishing touch.
