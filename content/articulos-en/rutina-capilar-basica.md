Unlike a skincare routine (where order gets debated a lot), the most common mistake in hair care is simpler: using everything together, every day, without understanding what each step actually does. This guide separates the functions and tells you when to use each product.

## Step 0: Before you wash (optional, not a required step)

This step isn't required like the next six, but it's the one that went most viral on TikTok in recent years under the #rosemaryoilforhair hashtag: an oil massaged into the scalp **before** you get in the shower, not after.

The [Mielle Organics Rosemary Mint Scalp & Hair Strengthening Oil](/en/productos/B07N7PK9QK) is the most-reviewed product in this category (122,000+ reviews, 4.5 stars) — with rosemary, mint, and biotin, you massage it into your scalp for 2-3 minutes and let it sit before washing with the shampoo from Step 1. To set the right expectations (and be upfront about the other side of the virality): the scientific evidence is still preliminary, and in 2023-2024 a viral TikTok video sparked a wave of hair-loss reports from very frequent use, which dermatologists attributed to irritation from the essential oils on sensitive scalps, not a flaw in the product itself. That's why it's worth patch-testing a small area first, as with any new essential oil.

## The most common mistake: using everything, every day

None of these steps replaces another, but you also don't need to use all of them at every wash. A realistic routine looks like this:

- **Every day:** cleansing + conditioning (steps 1-2)
- **Before heat:** heat protection (step 4)
- **1-2 times a week:** deep treatment (step 3) or the optional pre-wash oil (step 0) — not necessarily the same day
- **When needed:** frizz control (step 5) or dry shampoo between washes (step 6)

## Frequently asked questions

**Can I use the anti-dandruff shampoo and the hydrating conditioner together?**
Yes — the 2-in-1 shampoo already includes conditioner, but if your scalp ends up dry you can add the moisturizing conditioner as an extra step without issue.

**Does the heat-protecting oil replace the anti-frizz serum?**
Not entirely — the oil prioritizes heat protection and shine, the serum prioritizes frizz control in humid climates. You can use both: the oil before heat, the serum as a finishing touch.

**Is the pre-wash rosemary oil safe for everyone?**
For most people, yes, used 1-2 times a week. But if you have a sensitive scalp or notice itching, burning, or irritation, space out applications or stop using it — the viral 2023-2024 case that linked this type of product to hair loss was explained by contact dermatitis on reactive skin, not a problem with the oil itself. And if your hair loss is a real medical issue, this oil is a complement, not a treatment that replaces a dermatologist.

**Does the satin pillowcase replace the anti-frizz serum or the heat-protecting oil?**
No — they're different steps that complement each other. The serum and oil are applied to your hair and work during the day or before heat; the satin pillowcase isn't applied to anything, it just reduces friction while you sleep. You can (and should) use all three: oil before the blow dryer or iron, serum as a finishing touch, and the satin pillowcase every single night, no exceptions.

**Does the hair claw clip replace the heatless curling headband or the straightening brush?**
No, it solves a different problem. The headband and the straightener brush shape your hair (waves or straight) for a result that lasts the day or several days; the clip doesn't shape anything, it just holds hair up in the moment, with no change to its shape once you take it out. They're complementary, not competing: you can still straighten or curl with the tools above, and on the days you don't want to do either, reach for the clip for a five-second fix instead.

**Does the detangling brush replace the HEETA scalp massager from Step 1?**
No, they solve different problems. The HEETA works on the scalp (circulation, not the lengths) and is used during washing, with silicone bristles massaging; the UNbrush works on the length of your hair (knots, not the scalp) and is used before styling, wet or dry. You can use both in the same routine without overlap: the HEETA while rinsing out shampoo, the UNbrush afterward, getting out of the shower or before applying the heat protectant from Step 4.
