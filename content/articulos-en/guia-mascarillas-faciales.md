"Facial mask" isn't one single thing — there are at least three families with completely different mechanisms, and using the wrong one for what you need is the most common reason people end up disappointed with this type of product. This guide separates the three and tells you when to use each.

## 1. Clay — for cleansing and absorbing oil

Clay is the only one of the three that actually **cleans**: it dries on skin and, as it contracts, pulls excess oil and impurities out of pores. It doesn't hydrate — if anything, it can dry skin out if you leave it on too long or use it too often.

**Use it if:** you have oily skin, blackheads, or congested pores. **Frequency:** once a week, no more.

## 2. Hydrogel — for one-off intensive hydration

Hydrogel is a thick sheet that sticks to your whole face and releases a concentrated dose of hydration (and in some cases collagen) all at once. The "plumped skin" effect you see when you remove it is real, but temporary — it lasts 1-2 days.

**Use it if:** you have an important event the next day or want a weekly intensive-hydration treat. **Frequency:** 1-2 times a week, never daily.

## 3. Peel-off — for instant glow with a satisfying "reveal"

Unlike the other two, this one is applied as a thick cream, forms an elastic film as it dries (10-15 minutes), and peels off whole in one piece — it isn't rinsed off. The result is instant luminosity, not deep cleansing or long-term hydration.

**Use it if:** you want the instant "glow" effect before heading out, with the extra satisfaction of peeling it off whole. **Frequency:** once a week.

## How to choose without getting it wrong

| You need... | Use... |
|---|---|
| To clean congested pores | Clay |
| Intensive hydration before an event | Hydrogel |
| Quick glow, satisfying visual effect | Peel-off |

All three can coexist in your routine — they don't compete with each other because they solve different problems. What doesn't make sense is using all three the same week expecting your skin to "handle" that much active treatment at once.

## Frequently asked questions

**Can I use clay and hydrogel the same week?**
Yes — they actually pair well: clay to clean mid-week, hydrogel on the weekend to hydrate after the deep clean.

**Does a peel-off mask clean pores like clay does?**
No. Peel-off gives a visual luminosity effect, but it doesn't replace clay's deep-cleaning function.
