_Last updated: September 2, 2026._

This Privacy Policy describes how **BeautyLab** ("we," "our," or "the site") collects, uses, and shares information when you visit beautylab.com (the "Site").

## 1. Information we collect

- **Automatically collected usage information:** IP address, browser type, pages visited, time spent on the site, and similar data collected through cookies and analytics tools (see section 3).
- **Information you provide voluntarily:** your email address if you subscribe to download a guide or routine, or any information you include when contacting us.

We do not collect financial or payment information — all purchases are processed directly on Amazon.com under its own policies.

## 2. How we use the information

- Operate, maintain, and improve the Site.
- Send downloadable content to those who voluntarily subscribe (you can unsubscribe at any time).
- Analyze aggregate usage patterns to understand which content is useful.
- Comply with legal obligations.

## 3. Cookies and analytics

This Site uses **Google Analytics 4** to understand how content is used (pages visited, time on site, traffic source). Google Analytics uses first- and third-party cookies; you can opt out by installing the [Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on](https://tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout) or by rejecting cookies in your browser settings. Some features of the Site may be affected if you disable cookies.

## 4. Amazon Associates Program

**BeautyLab is a participant in the Amazon Services LLC Associates Program**, an affiliate advertising program designed to provide a means for sites to earn advertising fees by advertising and linking to Amazon.com (and, where applicable, other Amazon-affiliated domains).

When you click a product link on this Site and make a purchase on Amazon, we may receive a commission, at no extra cost to you. Amazon may place its own cookies in your browser when you click these links; Amazon's use of that information is governed by [Amazon's Privacy Policy](https://www.amazon.com/gp/help/customer/display.html?nodeId=468496).

## 5. Sharing data with third parties

We do not sell your personal information. We may share aggregated, non-identifiable data with analytics providers and with Amazon (through its own affiliate cookies) exclusively for the purposes described in this policy.

## 6. Links to third-party sites

This Site contains links to Amazon.com and, occasionally, to other external sites cited as references in our guides. We do not control the content or privacy practices of those sites; we recommend reviewing the privacy policy of any external site before providing it with personal information.

## 7. Data retention

We keep subscription information until the user unsubscribes. Analytics data is retained according to the default retention settings of the tool used.

## 8. Your rights

You can request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data (for example, your email address) by writing to us through the [Contact](/en/acerca-de) page. If you reside in the European Union, the European Economic Area, or California, you may have additional rights under the GDPR or CCPA (access, portability, deletion, and objection to processing); we honor them the same way for all users who request them, regardless of your location.

## 9. Children's privacy

This Site is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect information from minors.

## 10. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. The "last updated" date at the top of this page reflects the current version.

## 11. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, contact us through the [About](/en/acerca-de) page.
