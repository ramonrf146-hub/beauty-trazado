My name is Falcon. Before this site, I built [HidroLab](https://riegocom.uk) (residential irrigation) and [AutomatizaLab](https://controltrazado.com) (industrial automation and control) with the same approach: evaluate products by what they actually do, not by how much a brand paid to show up first. BeautyLab applies that same standard to skincare, sun protection, makeup, and lips — the part of everyday household spending that almost never gets evaluated with real criteria, only with "I saw it in a video."

## Why another site, and why separate

Beauty and personal care have nothing to do with automated irrigation or variable frequency drives — lumping everything into one site wouldn't serve anyone. That's why BeautyLab has its own catalog, its own domain, and its own editorial line, even though it shares the same underlying philosophy as its sister sites: real reviews before label promises.

## What you'll find here

- **An updated ranking** of the best-rated skincare, sun protection, makeup, and lip products on Amazon, based on real review data.
- **Honest notes** on every product — if something has fragrance when it claims not to, if a sunscreen leaves a white cast, or if a formula only works for one specific skin type, we say so.
- **Practical routines**, like the 5-minute everyday guide, connecting each step to the exact product that solves it.

## How the site is funded

BeautyLab participates in the Amazon Associates Program. This means some product links on this site are affiliate links: if you buy through them, we receive a small commission at no extra cost to you. This doesn't affect which products we recommend or how we evaluate them — selection is based on real reviews and data, not on which product pays the highest commission.
